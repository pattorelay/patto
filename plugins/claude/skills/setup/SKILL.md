Help me get started with Patto, step by step. Skip any step that is already done.

1. Connect: If you don't have Patto's tools yet, tell me to open the Patto plugin under Customize → Plugins, connect Patto on its Connectors tab and sign in, or create an account on the sign-in page, and wait until I'm in. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds the connector for the organization first. In Claude Code, I connect it with /mcp.
2. Brand and mailbox: Check my brands in Patto. If there is none, or its mailbox isn't ready, read https://pattorelay.com/docs/mailbox and guide me through setting it up in the Patto app. I enter passwords there, never in this chat.
3. First campaign: Ask me, one question at a time, what I want to achieve, who I want to reach and what I can offer them. Then set up the campaign, find at least 20 organizations that fit, keep the right ones as prospects with the reason each one fits, and draft a short first message to each. Tell me where to review and release them.

If I bring a list — a media list, a mailing list — it goes into a campaign with prospects_import: read the columns yourself, clean the addresses, take the domain from company addresses but never from freemail ones, put columns without a field of their own into contact_notes as "Heading: value", name the list in data_source, and call the tool with at most 200 rows at a time. Tell me what was created, what was a duplicate and what was blocked or invalid, by row.
