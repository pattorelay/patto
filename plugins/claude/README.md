# Patto

Patto gives your AI the structure and infrastructure for outreach: contacts, campaigns, follow-ups and your own mailboxes, with draft review and controlled sending.

Your AI researches, plans and writes. Patto stores the campaign brief and links the prospects and drafts, so a conversation turns into outreach and the next campaign starts from what the last one sent and what came back.

Replies are read as they arrive and answered as drafts inside their thread. Released drafts leave through your brand's own mail account, so the whole exchange stays in your mailbox.

Patto keeps organizations, contacts, prospect stages, follow-up dates and recorded interactions, so your assistant can pick up the working context in a later conversation. Each brand has its own sender identity, mailbox and campaigns.

## What this plugin contains

- One skill, `setup`, that walks you from the connection and your brand's mailbox to the first campaign.
- A reference to Patto's remote MCP server at `https://pattorelay.com/mcp`. Nothing runs on your machine.

When you connect, you sign in to Patto with OAuth and confirm the scopes Claude may use.

## Install

In Claude Code, run `/plugin install patto --marketplace pattorelay/patto`. In the Claude apps, open Customize → Plugins, install Patto and connect it on its Connectors tab.
